Gradienti & mattoni

I profili non sono "grigio": una rampa di luci/ombre da un solo colore (OKLCH). E il muro è un <pattern> di due gradienti.

<!-- profilo metallico = rampa derivata da un solo --fittings-color --> <linearGradient id="profileGradient" x1="0%" x2="100%"> <stop offset="0%" stop-color="oklch(from var(--fittings-color) calc(l * 0.8) c h)"/> <stop offset="45%" stop-color="oklch(from var(--fittings-color) max(0.3, calc(l * 1.08)) c h)"/> <stop offset="100%" stop-color="oklch(from var(--fittings-color) calc(l * 0.88) c h)"/> </linearGradient> <!-- il muro di mattoni: un <pattern> di due gradienti alternati --> <pattern id="brickPattern" width="0.2" height="0.1" patternUnits="objectBoundingBox"> <rect x="0" y="0" width="100" height="100" fill="url(#brickGradient1)"/> <rect x="100" y="0" width="100" height="100" fill="url(#brickGradient2)"/> <rect x="0" y="100" width="100" height="100" fill="url(#brickGradient2)"/> <rect x="100" y="100" width="100" height="100" fill="url(#brickGradient1)"/> </pattern> ›
